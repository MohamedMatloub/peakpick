Photos access

PeakPick AI needs Photos permission to scan your library and, when you confirm cleanup, move selected photos to Recently Deleted.

If access was denied or limited, open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PeakPick AI and choose the access level you want to allow.

Scanning and iCloud Photos

Photo comparisons are performed on your device. Photos that are stored in iCloud may need to download before they can be analyzed. Scanning can take longer when images are only available in iCloud or when network access is restricted.

Cleanup and Recently Deleted

PeakPick AI asks you to review photos before cleanup. Confirmed deletions are handled through Apple Photos and are normally placed in Recently Deleted. Apple controls how long items remain there and when they are permanently removed.

PeakPick AI Pro and purchase restoration

PeakPick AI Pro unlocks the paid app features described on the purchase screen. Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and validated using RevenueCat.

If you purchased PeakPick AI Pro but the feature is locked:

  1. Confirm that you are using the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
  2. Open the app's Settings and choose Restore Purchase.
  3. Make sure your device has an internet connection, then try again.
  4. If the problem continues, contact us with the approximate purchase date and the Apple transaction details. Do not send your payment-card information.

For billing, refund, or App Store transaction questions, Apple may require you to use its Report a Problem service.

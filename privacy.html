Privacy Policy for PeakPick AI: Photo Cleaner

Effective date: 28 August 2026
Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mohamed Matloub ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use PeakPick AI: Photo Cleaner (the "App").

1. Summary

PeakPick AI is designed to help you find similar and duplicate photos in your Apple Photos library. Photo analysis is performed on your device. We do not operate an account system and do not intentionally upload your photo images, thumbnails, or photo-library metadata to our own servers.

The App uses Apple's PhotoKit framework to access the photos you authorize. If a photo is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple may download the required image data to your device when PhotoKit is allowed to use the network. Apple's handling of iCloud Photos is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

2. Information the App accesses and stores locally

When you grant Photos access, the App may access:

  • Photo-library identifiers and dates.
  • Image dimensions and file-size information when available.
  • Photo attributes such as favorite, edited, Live Photo, screenshot, panorama, and burst status.
  • Location metadata associated with a photo, when provided by PhotoKit.
  • Image data needed to create local comparisons, quality assessments, thumbnails, and similarity results.

The App may store related information locally on your device, including scan results, photo identifiers, grouping and ranking results, progress checkpoints, cached analysis data or thumbnails, settings, cleanup history, and notification preferences. This information is used to provide the App's features and is not intentionally transmitted to us.

You control Photos permission through iOS Settings. You can revoke access at any time, although some App features may then stop working.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

The App uses Apple's App Store payment system and RevenueCat to process and validate the optional PeakPick AI Pro purchase and maintain its entitlement status.

RevenueCat may process purchase and subscription information, an anonymous App User ID, device and operating-system information, app activity needed to maintain purchase status, and Apple transaction or receipt information. We do not receive or store your payment-card number. Payments are handled by Apple.

RevenueCat's processing is governed by its own privacy policy, available at revenuecat.com/privacy-policy. Apple services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

We do not use RevenueCat to identify you by name or email unless we later introduce an account or customer-information feature and explain that change in an updated policy.

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App may schedule local reminders about unfinished or available photo scans. Notification preferences and scheduled notifications are managed by iOS and the App on your device. We do not use notifications to collect advertising identifiers or to send marketing messages.

5. Information you provide to support

If you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name, device details, App version, and the contents of your message. We use this information only to respond to you, investigate an issue, and maintain support records.

Support contact: peakpickai.support@gmail.com

Please do not send photographs or other sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to explain your support request.

6. Information we do not intentionally collect

As of the effective date, the App does not include our own account database, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting service. We do not intentionally collect your photo images, photo thumbnails, precise location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifier for our own purposes.

This statement does not override information processed by Apple, RevenueCat, or other third parties operating their own services. We will update this policy if the App's data practices change.

7. How information is used

We use information to:

  • Provide photo scanning, grouping, ranking, review, and cleanup features.
  • Remember scan progress, settings, and local results.
  • Validate purchases and unlock eligible App features.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect the App, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or use your photos for advertising.

8. Sharing and service providers

We may share limited information with service providers only when needed to operate the App:

  • Apple, for Photos access, iCloud Photos, App Store payments, and related system services.
  • RevenueCat, for purchase validation and entitlement management.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. We do not share photo content with these providers for advertising.

9. Retention and deletion

Local scan data and cached App data remain on your device until the App removes them, you use an available in-App cleanup option, or you delete the App and iOS removes its local data. Photos moved to Recently Deleted are controlled by Apple Photos and remain subject to Apple's retention and deletion behavior.

Because the App does not require an account, we generally cannot associate local photo-library data with your identity. To ask about support records or purchase-related information, contact peakpickai.support@gmail.com. We may need to coordinate with Apple or RevenueCat for information held by those providers.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also:

  • Change or revoke Photos permission in iOS Settings.
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings or in the App, where available.
  • Delete the App and its locally stored App data.
  • Contact us at peakpickai.support@gmail.com with a privacy request.

We may need to verify your request and may retain information where required by law or needed to resolve a dispute.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact peakpickai.support@gmail.com.

Apple and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their own privacy notices describe their processing and applicable safeguards.
